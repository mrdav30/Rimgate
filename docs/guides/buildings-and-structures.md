# Buildings and Structures

This page is a player-facing overview of Rimgate construction options: what each building family is for, when to use it, and what to prioritize in base planning.

For gate operation details, see [Gate Network Guide](gate-network-guide.md).  
For deep system loops, see [Symbiotes and Prim'ta](symbiotes-and-primta.md), [Cloning Systems](cloning-systems.md), and [Sarcophagus Guide](sarcophagus-guide.md).

## At a Glance

- Rimgate adds strong building identity by faction.
- `Tau'ri` buildings focus on practical infrastructure, research support, modular defense, and logistics tools.
- `Goa'uld` buildings focus on heavy fortification and intimidation architecture.
- `Wraith` buildings focus on biotech infrastructure and organic construction.
- `Ancient` structures provide high-impact artifact and power tech.
- Several buildings are progression assets, not just furniture (DHD/ZPM analysis unlocks, ZPM masking, shield infrastructure).
- The fastest base growth usually comes from reliable power first, then research/production acceleration, then layered defense.

## Quick Build Priorities

| Stage | Priorities | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early transition | `naquadria generator` or `biomass generator`, `composition bench`, basic barriers/fences | Stabilizes power and gives immediate crafting/defense options. |
| Mid progression | `M2 turret`, `tracked cart`/`refrigerated tracked cart`, `tau'ri research desk` + `blueprint table` | Improves raid handling, hauling throughput, and research tempo. |
| Late infrastructure | `ZPM containment housing`, `subspace shroud emitter`, `ancient shield emitter`, `durable walls/doors` | Enables high-end grids and fortress-grade survivability. |

## Power, Shielding, and Climate

| Building | Role | Practical Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `naquadria generator` | Compact high-output generator | Strong output for footprint, but volatile and fuel-dependent. |
| `industrial naquadria generator` | Very high-output plant | Excellent megabase power anchor with significant risk and build cost. |
| `biomass generator` | Wraith fuel power | Efficient biomass conversion and solid mid-tier output. |
| `ZPM containment housing` | Multi-ZPM grid core | Endgame battery/manifold for loaded ZPM stacks. |

| `subspace phase diverter` | ZPM support utility | Improves long-run ZPM sustainability through subspace coupling. |
| `ancient shield emitter` | Base-wide projectile defense | Extremely power-hungry but high-impact against ranged threats. |
| `climate crystal` | Tok'ra temp control | Fuel-based climate stabilizer for difficult biome control. |

## Defense and Fortification

### Defensive structures

| Structure | Role | Practical Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `military barrier` / `concrete barrier` | Cover and lane shaping | Fast-value perimeter control and firing line support. |
| `Goa'uld wall` / `hive wall` | Themed core wall sets | Durable alternatives with faction flavor and strong utility. |
| `chain-link fence` / `concrete slab fence` (+ gate) | Perimeter management | Good for controlled movement without full hard-wall commitment. |
| `durable wall` + `durable doors` | Fortress-grade shell | Extremely durable and intentionally hard to remove once placed. |

### Turret families

| Turret | Role | Practical Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `manned M2 .50 cal` | Long-range sustained ballistic defense | Needs operator plus barrel/ammo-style upkeep. |
| `manned naquadah enhanced railgun` | Extreme-range anti-armor fire support | High construction and ammo resource demand, very long reach. |
| `manned staff cannon` | Goa'uld heavy plasma defense | Strong burst pressure with naquadah-fed upkeep. |
| `auto staff cannon` | Autonomous heavy defense | Uses both power and fuel; ideal as anchor emplacements. |
| `auto staff cannon (shielded)` | Elite autonomous turret | More power-intensive, higher survivability in sustained sieges. |

### Encounter-only security features

- `force field`, `aegis door`, `jammed blast door`, and `secret door` appear mainly as map/quest obstacles, not routine base builds.
- `proximity alarm` is an encounter trap system tied to hostile stasis defenses.

| Cart | Role | Practical Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `tracked cart` | Mid-tier logistics mover | Better mass and handling with chemfuel operation. |

## Ships and Transport Platforms

`Tel'tak`, `Dart`, and `Puddle Jumper` are defined as buildable shuttle structures and use dedicated launch/refuel systems.

- Treat them as a separate transport gameplay layer rather than normal base furniture.
- They are best planned as a late-mid to endgame mobility package with fuel logistics in place.

## World and Loot Structures

- `naquadah ore` is the core mineable alien resource node.
- Many `Prop` defs are encounter or quest content (ruined vehicles, ancient vats, supply crates, cocoon pods, urns, and hazard props).
  - These are generally not your regular build menu progression, but they matter for map exploration and loot loops.

## Strategy Checklist

- Build power redundancy before committing to shield-heavy or ZPM-heavy infrastructure.
- Place research support facilities early in your main lab room for long-run value.
- Use barriers/fences to force enemy lanes before investing in expensive turret tiers.
- Do not overbuild durable walls/doors until your layout is stable, since they are hard to remove.
- Treat mobile carts as labor multipliers and assign them to your busiest logistics corridors.
- Keep deep system buildings centralized so future specialized guides and room layouts stay clean.
