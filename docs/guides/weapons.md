# Weapons

This page is a player-facing guide to Rimgate weapons: what each weapon family does well, when to field it, and what research gates matter most.

For armor pairings, see [Apparel and Utilities](apparel-and-utilities.md). For unlock flow, see [Research and Progression](research-and-progression.md).

## At a Glance

- Rimgate weapon identity is faction-driven:
  - `Tau'ri`: ballistic flexibility and role-specialized firearms.
  - `Goa'uld`: control weapons (stun/EMP) plus elite energy repeaters.
  - `Wraith`: bio-energy weapons with strong disable pressure.
- Several weapon lines are not just combat upgrades, they are progression gates.
- You get the best results by assigning weapons to job roles, not by giving everyone the same gun.

## Tau'ri Weapons

| Weapon | Role | Why You Use It | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `P90` | Close-mid suppressive fire | Fast 6-round bursts and quick handling for aggressive pushes. | Falls off at longer ranges. |
| `M4A1` | General-purpose rifle | Strong all-around range and accuracy profile for default line infantry. | Not specialized at either extreme. |
| `M4A1 w/ M203 Launcher` | Flexible rifleman | Can switch between rifle mode and launcher mode in the field. | Launcher mode is shorter-range and slower. |
| `M203 underbarrel launcher` | Anti-cover / anti-clump burst | Explosive 40mm shot with splash radius for clustered targets. | Less accurate than rifle mode and slower cycle. |
| `Beretta M92A` | Sidearm / backup gun | Very fast warmup makes it responsive on non-primary shooters. | Limited stopping power. |
| `USAS 12` | Breach and close defense | Strong close-range burst pressure in doorways and chokepoints. | Short effective range. |
| `Remington 700` | Long-range precision | Very high long-range accuracy for clean picks before enemies close. | Slow cadence and weaker in close fights. |
| `FIM Stinger` | Heavy explosive specialist | Large blast profile for high-value grouped targets. | Extremely long warmup; poor for reactive fights. |

### M4A1/M203 switch mechanic

- The combo rifle has a direct toggle command to swap between rifle and grenade launcher modes.
- Mode swapping preserves weapon quality and hit points.
- Best practice: leave it in rifle mode by default, then swap just before clumped or entrenched targets.

## Goa'uld Weapons

| Weapon | Role | Why You Use It | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ceremonial knife` | Early melee sidearm | Cheap, accessible melee option for Jaffa-leaning loadouts. | Outscaled by advanced melee tech later. |
| `Tok'Kal` | Crowd control grenade | Stun-focused AoE with EMP follow-up, strong for captures and mech disruption. | Poor lethality by itself. |
| `Ma'Tok staff` | Mid-range energy staff | Respectable ranged strike plus melee fallback in one weapon. | Single-shot cadence; less burst pressure than rifles. |
| `Zat'nik'tel` | Biological disable sidearm | Repeated hits can escalate into lethal shutdown on flesh targets. | Very weak against mechanical targets. |
| `plasma repeater` | Elite shock troop gun | Strong burst damage per volley for aggressive frontline shooters. | Accuracy drops hard at medium/long range. |
| `Rod of Anguish` | Rare control melee relic | Applies strong stun/EMP pressure in melee. | Not standard craft flow; extremely limited availability. |

### Kull analysis gate (important)

- `Kull weapons` research requires a recovered `plasma repeater` to be analyzed.
- Analysis can catastrophically fail and explode, potentially forcing another attempt.
- Use a high-Intellectual colonist and perform analysis in a safe room away from fragile gear.

## Wraith Weapons

| Weapon | Role | Why You Use It | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bio-grenade` | Area denial and opening pressure | Explosive opening tool for flushing targets from cover. | Not precision-safe for tight friendly lines. |
| `Wraith spear` | Mid-range disable shot | Single-shot projectile with added stun pressure, great for capture teams. | Lower raw DPS than burst guns. |
| `Wraith handblaster` | Short-mid skirmish blaster | Fast 3-shot pattern with burn + disable pressure. | Less consistent at long range. |
| `assassin blade` | Elite melee finisher | High armor penetration with poison follow-up for lethal close combat. | Requires committing to melee engagement. |

## Anti-Replicator Weapon

`Anti-Replicator gun` is a specialist sidegrade, not a universal upgrade:

- It is tuned to mechanoid/replicator-style targets.
- Against organics, it is intentionally poor value compared to standard firearms.
- Best use: dedicated anti-mech pawn with a normal sidearm or squad backup.

## Unique Weapon Variants

Unique versions exist for several weapon families (for example `P90`, `M4A1`, `USAS 12`, `Remington 700`, `Ma'Tok`, `plasma repeater`, `Wraith spear`, and `Wraith handblaster`).

- Treat these as high-value rewards and prestige upgrades rather than routine production items.
- They are best assigned to your most reliable shooters, not distributed randomly.

## Practical Role Pairings

- Tau'ri line rifleman: `M4A1` or `M4A1 w/ M203`.
- Tau'ri breacher: `USAS 12` or `P90`.
- Tau'ri marksman: `Remington 700`.
- Goa'uld control specialist: `Zat'nik'tel` + `Tok'Kal` support from another pawn.
- Goa'uld elite pusher: `plasma repeater` on a durable frontline shooter.
- Wraith capture team: `Wraith spear` and `Wraith handblaster`.
- Anti-mech specialist: `Anti-Replicator gun` backed by conventional squad weapons.

## Strategy Checklist

- Match weapon role to pawn role; avoid one-size-fits-all armories.
- Use `M203`, `Tok'Kal`, and `bio-grenade` to break cover and shape enemy movement.
- Keep `Zat'nik'tel` on targets you want disabled or captured, not mechs.
- Build around `plasma repeater` with shorter engagement lanes where its burst profile shines.
- Reserve `Anti-Replicator gun` for mech-heavy maps and incidents.
- Prioritize unique weapons on high-skill pawns who will actually land shots.
