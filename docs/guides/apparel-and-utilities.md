# Apparel and Utilities

This page is a player-facing guide to Rimgate wearable gear: faction armor sets, utility pieces, and how to combine them into strong loadouts.

For weapon pairing, see [Weapons](weapons.md). For research unlock flow, see [Research and Progression](research-and-progression.md).

## At a Glance

- Apparel is heavily faction-themed:
  - `Tau'ri`: modular tactical kits and precision utility.
  - `Goa'uld`: heavy intimidation armor with elite variants.
  - `Wraith`: biotech gear with essence and hacking synergy.
- Several top-tier utility pieces are also research gates via analyzable items.
- Tau'ri and Goa'uld both have strong upgrade ladders from standard to elite gear.
- Some items are powerful but have real tradeoffs (for example speed loss, shooting penalties, or weaker open-face variants).

## Tau'ri Modular System (Important)

Tau'ri apparel uses a custom compatibility system centered on vest "frame roots":

- `utility tactical vest` and `spec ops vest` are frame roots.
- If a vest-dependent item is worn without a valid vest, it auto-unequips.
- If you remove the vest, dependent modules auto-unequip.

### Vest-dependent modules

- `Plate inserts`:
  - `steel plate insert` and `composite plate insert`: attach to tactical or spec ops vest.
  - `naquadah plate insert`: spec ops vest only.
- `tactical backpack`: works with tactical or spec ops vest.
- `Vest attachments` (waist modules):
  - Early set (`construction`, `fishing`, `electronics`, `gardening`) uses tactical vest requirement.
  - Late set (`medic bag`, `ammo pouch`, `melee pouch`) supports tactical and spec ops vests.

Practical slot note:

- Vest attachments are waist-slot modules, so treat them as one-at-a-time picks.
- Backpack and waist module can usually coexist as long as their occupied body-part groups do not conflict with other worn utilities.

## Goa'uld Gear Guide

### Progression tiers

| Tier | Unlock | What You Get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jaffa core | `Jaffa armor` | Jaffa armor + helmet + boots + gauntlets baseline set |
| Prime/Guard tier | `Goa'uld guard armor` | First Prime upgrades and sealed guard helmet families |
| Elite tier | `Kull armor` and `Kara kesh` | Top Goa'uld armor tech and advanced utility |

### Notable Goa'uld mechanics

- `Kull armor`:
  - Extremely high protection and integrated shield behavior.
  - Very heavy movement penalty; ideal for anchor/tank roles, not mobile skirmishers.
- `Kull helmet`:
  - Strong head protection with toxic/vacuum capability.
- `Kara kesh`:
  - Utility hand device with shielding and strong support-style stat boosts.
  - Also an analyzable progression gate, so looted examples are strategically valuable.
- Guard helmet families (`Horus`, `Jackal`, `Serpent`):
  - Closed variants are sealed and stronger for hostile environments.
  - Open variants are style/flexibility variants with weaker protection.
  - Toggle support lets players switch between open/closed where applicable.

## Wraith Gear Guide

### Core armor path

| Item | Main Role | Unlock Tier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `drone helm` | Sealed combat helm | `Wraith warrior armor` | Strong all-around head protection with toxic resilience. |
| `chitin breastplate` | Mainline torso armor | `Wraith warrior armor` | Solid armor with strong cold profile for Wraith frontliners. |
| `armored overcoat` | Commander armor | `Wraith commander armor` | Better elite profile plus essence-focused bonuses. |
| `spacer bracers` | Arms utility/combat hybrid | `Wraith warrior armor` | Aiming and essence-oriented support piece. |
| `knee-high leather boots` | Mobility/field boots | `Wraith warrior armor` | Movement and stealth-leaning utility. |

### Wraith elite utility pieces

- `essence circlet`:
  - Leadership/psy-sensitive utility piece.
  - Supports social/trade influence and essence economy.
- `queen's pendant`:
  - Neck utility with lighter but similar leadership/essence support profile.
- `Wraith communication device`:
  - Mech bandwidth/control and strong hacking buffs.
  - Requires analyzable progression: you typically need to recover one before full production.
- `force buckler`:
  - Left-hand shield weapon hybrid.
  - Excellent for melee-forward assassins; not ideal for shooters due to accuracy penalty.

## Practical Loadout Patterns

- Tau'ri marksman:
  - `spec ops vest` + `naquadah plate insert` + `tactical gunlink` + `ammo pouch`.
- Tau'ri field medic:
  - `utility tactical vest` + `composite/steel insert` + `ballistic gasmask` + `medic bag`.
- Goa'uld elite tank:
  - `Kull armor` + `Kull helmet`.
- Wraith commander:
  - `armored overcoat` + `essence circlet` or `queen's pendant` + `Wraith communication device`.
- Wraith assassin:
  - `force buckler` + mobility-focused Wraith pieces; avoid shooter role overlap.

## Strategy Checklist

- Treat analyzable wearable tech as progression assets, not just loot-value items.
- Build Tau'ri kits around role specialization rather than one "best" module for everyone.
- Use closed Goa'uld guard helmets for toxic/vacuum operations; open versions for style or non-hazard maps.
- Assign Kull armor to slow frontline anchors, not reposition-heavy pawns.
- Pair Wraith essence gear on pawns who actually use Wraith abilities to realize full value.
