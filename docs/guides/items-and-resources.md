# Items and Resources

This guide covers the core non-weapon, non-apparel item economy in Rimgate: what each resource does, where it usually comes from, and how it fits into progression.

For dialing/exploration item flow, also see [Gate Network Guide](gate-network-guide.md). For unlock gates, see [Research and Progression](research-and-progression.md).

## At a Glance

- `naquadah` is the backbone material for most advanced Rimgate gear, buildings, and power systems.
- `naquadria` is high-value and volatile: great output, high risk.
- Wraith economy runs on `cultured meat` -> `cocooned meat` -> `biomass`.
- Several key progression unlocks come from analyzable items (not normal bench research alone).
- Some utility items are intentionally single-use and should be treated as strategic tools, not everyday consumables.

## Core Resource Loops

1. `naquadah` -> `naquadria`
   - Mine naquadah ore veins, deep-drill it, loot it, or trade for it.
   - Analyze a naquadah sample, then unlock `refine naquadah` research and refine at a fabrication bench.
2. `meat` -> `cocooned meat` -> `biomass`
   - Grow `cultured meat` in a Meat Lab or process raw meat.
   - Cocoon and process at a Cocoon Spot to produce biomass for Wraith systems.
3. `stone` -> `cement` -> `concrete`
   - Smelt stone/chunks into cement, then mix into concrete for concrete-based structures.
   - Concrete is time-sensitive and temperature-sensitive once made.

## Strategic Materials

| Resource | Main Use | Common Sources | Important Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `naquadah` | Core alien-tech material across factions | Naquadah ore, deep drilling, gate-site loot, some traders, meteorite incident | Also analyzable for `refine naquadah`; used heavily in advanced construction and crafting. |
| `naquadria` | High-tier fuel/reactive material | Refined from Naquadah (fab bench), rare loot, Tau'ri traders | Extremely valuable and explosive; store safely away from heat/fire lines. |
| `inert replicator link` | Replicator tech progression and mech recipes | Butchered from replicator enemies, some combat salvage | Can be analyzed (Mechanitor required) or smelted into steel if you need quick metal. |
| `biomass` | Wraith crafting, biotech systems, power chains | Cocoon/meat conversion at Cocoon Spot, Wraith loot/traders | Core Wraith production currency; keep a steady conversion bill. |
| `cement` | Intermediate for concrete economy | Smelted from stone blocks/chunks | Industrial conversion step; plan stockpiles before mass concrete production. |
| `concrete` | Concrete walls, barriers, and related structures | Mixed from cement + stone chunks | Dissolves/hardens over time and is destroyed if frozen. Use it quickly. |

## Practical Strategy Checklist

- Lock in a reliable `naquadah` source early (ore veins, deep drill, or trade), then protect it like advanced components.
- Keep analyzable key items in a safe lab and use strong Intellectual pawns to reduce catastrophic losses.
- Do not bulk-store `naquadria` near critical infrastructure.
- If running Wraith systems, automate cocoon/biomass bills early so power and production do not stall.
- Treat `Tretonin` as life-support inventory once symbiote surgery workflows begin.
- Use `tunnel crystals` on high-value choke excavations, not routine mining.
- Hold `glyph parchment`, `cipher fragments`, and rare bio-artifacts until your combat team is ready to capitalize on the content they unlock.
